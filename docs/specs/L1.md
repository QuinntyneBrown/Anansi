# Anansi Platform - L1: High-Level Requirements

> Anansi is an all-in-one platform for photographers, with future
> customization for Toronto Black photographers. This document defines the high-level feature
> areas and capabilities the platform must deliver.

---

## 1. Client Galleries

The core photo/video delivery and proofing system for sharing work with clients.

### 1.1 Photo & Video Upload
- Browser-based drag-and-drop upload (photos and videos)
- Bulk upload via folder drag-and-drop (auto-creates collections/sets)
- Lightroom Classic plugin for direct publishing and re-publishing
- Support for JPEG, PNG, GIF, RAW, and video formats (MP4, MOV, AVI, M4V)
- Up to 4K video upload and playback

### 1.2 Gallery Organization
- Collections and sub-sets within collections
- Bulk gallery creation with preset settings (for mini sessions, schools, events)
- Star/bookmark collections and photos for quick access
- Sorting and arrangement controls within collections
- Collection presets (save preferred settings as reusable templates)

### 1.3 Gallery Design & Customization
- Multiple cover styles (7+) with photo or video covers (YouTube/Vimeo embed)
- Light and dark theme toggle
- Font selection (6+ curated options)
- Color palette selection (9+ options)
- Vertical and horizontal grid layouts
- Custom set titles and descriptions
- Slideshow mode with adjustable speed and auto-loop

### 1.4 Photo Delivery & Downloads
- Full gallery or individual photo downloads
- Multiple download resolutions (web size: 640px/1024px/2048px; high-res: 3600px/original)
- 4-digit download PIN per collection
- Per-collection download limits
- Async download with email notification when ready
- Download activity tracking (who, when, what)

### 1.5 Proofing & Favorites
- Client-created favorite lists (one or more per collection)
- Comments and edit requests on favorited images
- Photographer-preset favorite list categories
- Configurable favorite count limits
- Favorite list sharing, downloading, and digital delivery to any email
- Favorites visible in Lightroom via plugin
- Favorite activity dashboard

### 1.6 Privacy & Access Control
- Collection password protection
- Client Exclusive Access (separate password with expanded set visibility)
- Homepage password protection
- Email registration gate (require email before viewing)
- Private photos (client-marked, hidden from all other viewers)
- Collection expiration with auto-hide
- Automated expiry reminder emails

### 1.7 Sharing & Social
- Email invitations with customizable branded templates
- Social media sharing (Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest, WhatsApp, Messenger, Threads)
- Quick Share via unique link (for vendors, collaborators)
- QR code generation for gallery sharing
- Gallery embedding on external websites

### 1.8 Multi-Language Support
- 8+ languages for all client-facing gallery elements and automated emails
- Per-collection language setting

### 1.9 Gallery Activity & Analytics
- Activities tab per collection (downloads, favorites, private photos, email registrations)
- Google Analytics integration for galleries
- Real-time notification center for gallery events

## 4. Studio Manager (CRM & Business Tools)

Complete business management, client relationships, and workflow automation.

### 4.1 Contacts & CRM
- Contact types: Client, Lead, Other
- Centralized contact profiles (details, documents, emails, sessions, galleries)
- Automatic lead-to-client conversion on booking/payment
- Contact import from external sources
- Lead capture via custom contact forms (embeddable, shareable via link)
- Custom form fields (short text, long text, multiple choice, checkboxes, date/time)

### 4.2 Projects (Pipeline Board)
- Visual Kanban-style project board
- Customizable workflow stages (add, remove, rename, reorder)
- Drag-and-drop project cards between stages
- Each project groups documents, sessions, and payments
- Auto-created project cards for new form submissions

### 4.3 Booking & Scheduling
- Online booking site (branded, customizable)
- Full session types with configurable duration, pricing, availability
- Mini session types with date slots, start times, gaps/breaks
- Calendar view for managing all sessions
- Google Calendar two-way sync with conflict detection
- Buffer time between sessions (pre and post)
- Advance booking limits
- Manual confirmation mode (accept/decline requests)
- Session visibility control (public or hidden/link-only)
- Intake documents during booking (contracts + questionnaires)
- Payment/deposit collection at booking time (full or split installments)
- Video call integration (Zoom, Google Meet)
- Booking coupons and promotional codes
- Embeddable booking widget
- "Nearly sold out" urgency indicator for mini sessions

### 4.4 Contracts & E-Signatures
- Rich content contract editor
- Client-fillable fields (initials, addresses, custom info)
- Auto-population variables (client name, email, phone, etc.)
- Custom variables prompted at template application
- E-signature collection on any device
- Multi-signer support (e.g., second signer for spouse/partner)
- Photographer pre-signing
- Contract templates (save and reuse) with sample templates included
- Document expiry (auto-cancel if unsigned by deadline)
- Automatic reminder emails for unsigned contracts
- Custom header images for branding

### 4.5 Invoicing & Payments
- Invoice builder with line items (name, description, quantity, price)
- Payment schedules with multiple installments and due dates
- Deposit/retainer collection
- Scheduled invoice send dates
- Automatic payment reminders (before, on, after due date)
- Tax support on invoices
- Gratuity/tip option (preset percentages or custom amount)
- Custom invoice header fields (VAT, ABN, etc.)
- Invoice templates
- Auto-generate invoice draft from accepted quotes

### 4.6 Quotes / Proposals
- Create and send price quotes with selectable services
- Auto-generate invoice draft when client accepts a quote
- Quote templates

### 4.7 Questionnaires
- Drag-and-drop question builder
- Question types: short text, long text, multiple choice, checkboxes, date, email
- Required field toggle per question
- Questionnaire templates (save and reuse) with sample templates included
- Standalone sending or as booking intake documents
- Public sharing option for multiple submissions
- Document expiry and automatic reminders

### 4.8 Payment Processing
- Credit/debit cards
- Digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link)
- Buy Now Pay Later (Klarna, Affirm)
- Bank transfers (ACH)
- PayPal
- Tap to Pay (in-person contactless via mobile app)
- Offline payment recording (cash, check)
- Instant payouts option
- Standard payout timeline: 2-3 business days
- Transaction fee: 2.9% + $0.30 per card/wallet transaction

### 4.9 Gift Cards
- Custom gift cards with stored dollar amounts
- Optional expiry dates
- Delivery via branded email or manual code
- Redeemable toward invoices or booking checkout
- Leftover balance saved for future use

### 4.10 Financial Reporting
- Dashboard with revenue graphs and charts
- Metrics: total revenue, net revenue, payment method breakdown, tips, taxes
- Date range filtering (yearly, quarterly, monthly)
- Transaction breakdown with net profit
- Export full payment data for tax/accounting
- Track paid and pending invoices

### 4.11 Business Financing (Capital)
- Business financing for eligible photographers
- Direct bank deposit of funds
- Automatic repayment via percentage of incoming payments
- No credit check, no impact on personal credit
- One-time flat fee (no compounding interest)

## 20. Interac e-Transfer Integration

Support for Interac e-Transfer as a payment method alongside Stripe and PayPal. Photographers can send Interac payment requests directly from invoices. The system tracks pending e-Transfer payments and reconciles them when the photographer confirms receipt. Clients see Interac e-Transfer as a familiar Canadian payment option at checkout and on invoices.

### 20.1 Interac Payment Requests
- Generate Interac e-Transfer payment requests from invoices
- Include auto-fill email, amount, and payment reference in the request
- Track payment request status (Pending, Completed, Expired)
- Photographer confirms receipt to reconcile payment against invoice

### 20.2 Interac Payment Method in Checkout
- Interac e-Transfer option on store checkout and invoice payment pages
- Display photographer's registered Interac email for manual transfer
- Payment reference code generation for reconciliation
- Instructions for clients on completing the transfer

## 21. HST Calculator & Threshold Tracker

Built-in Ontario HST (13%) support for Canadian photographers. Automatic HST calculation on invoices and quotes. Revenue tracking against the $30,000 CRA mandatory registration threshold across rolling four-quarter periods. Alerts when approaching the threshold. Input Tax Credit tracking for business equipment purchases.

### 21.1 Tax Profile Configuration
- Enable/disable HST collection with registration number
- Set HST rate (default 13% for Ontario, configurable for other provinces)
- Store HST registration number (Business Number)
- Track voluntary vs mandatory registration status

### 21.2 HST on Invoices & Quotes
- Automatic HST line-item calculation on invoices
- HST displayed as separate line item with registration number
- HST applied to quotes with carry-through to generated invoices
- Tax-exempt line items supported

### 21.3 Revenue Threshold Tracker
- Rolling four-quarter revenue accumulator
- Dashboard widget showing progress toward $30,000 threshold
- Alert notifications at 75% and 90% of threshold
- Quarterly revenue breakdown view

### 21.4 Input Tax Credit Tracking
- Log business expenses with HST paid
- Categorize expenses (equipment, software, studio, travel, supplies)
- Calculate net HST owing (collected minus ITCs)
- Export ITC summary for CRA filing

### 23.1 Preset Data Model

### 23.2 Preset Browsing & Management
- Browse presets by skin tone range and context

### 23.3 Preset CRUD for Contributors
